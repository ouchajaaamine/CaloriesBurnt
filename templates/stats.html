Personalized tips

Optimal duration

Exercises of 20-30 minutes show the best calories/time ratio. Prioritize sessions of this duration to maximize efficiency.

Target heart rate zone

Maintain a heart rate between 100-115 BPM to maximize fat burning. Use a heart rate monitor to stay in this zone.

Consistency

Regular 15-20 minute sessions are more effective than a single long session per week. Aim for 3-4 sessions per week for optimal results.

Body temperature

A slight increase in body temperature (up to 39°C) is associated with better calorie burning. Include a 5-minute warm-up.
